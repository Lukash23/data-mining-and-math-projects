# Calculus

Numerical and symbolic calculus explorations in Python, written for a university course.

| Notebook | What it covers |
|---|---|
| `project1_functions_and_secant_slopes.ipynb` | Function domains and plots, and secant-line slopes approaching a point on a sine curve to explore limits and tangents |
| `project2_limits_and_fixed_point.ipynb` | Epsilon-delta limits, plots of discontinuities, and a fixed-point iteration solver with a tolerance |
| `project3_derivatives_sympy.ipynb` | Derivatives with NumPy and SymPy, simplification, horizontal and vertical tangents, and extrema and inflection points of Gaussian-style functions |
| `project4_roots_and_integration.ipynb` | Polynomial roots (graphical and with `numpy.roots`), area under a curve with a Riemann sum compared against SymPy's exact integral, and local extrema of the sine integral |

**Tools:** Python, NumPy, SciPy, SymPy, Matplotlib
